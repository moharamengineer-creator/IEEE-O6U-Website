Our Specialized Teams

Discover the diverse fields and committees at IEEE O6U Student Branch.

HR Team

Human Resources: Responsible for organizing the administrative structure, monitoring member performance, and managing recruitment and evaluation processes.

PR Team

Public Relations: The link between the branch and the external world, responsible for partnerships, sponsors, and official communications.

OP Team

Operations: The masterminds behind logistics and event management, ensuring everything runs perfectly on the ground.

Graphic Design

Responsible for the branch's visual identity, designing posts and logos, and transforming creative ideas into stunning visuals.

Robotics

Diving deep into the world of robotics, from mechanical design and electronic circuits to programming and control systems.

Biomedical

Integrating engineering and medicine to develop medical devices and understand modern biotechnology.

AI Team

Artificial Intelligence: Exploring Machine Learning, data processing, and building intelligent algorithms for future tech.

Automotive

Focusing on vehicle engineering and automotive technology, including modern engine systems and dynamics.

Automation

Studying automated systems in industry and learning how to make processes operate autonomously and efficiently.

Aerospace

Aerospace and Aviation science: studying aircraft design and systems that operate outside the atmosphere.

Classic Control

The fundamentals of classical control, electrical circuits, motor control, and traditional industrial processes.
