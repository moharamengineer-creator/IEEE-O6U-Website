Who We Are

IEEE O6U is a student branch at October 6 University, part of the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. We aim to bridge the gap between academic study and the professional engineering world.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide students with the resources, environment, and opportunities to enhance their technical knowledge and professional skills.

💡

Our Vision

To be the leading student community for innovation, fostering engineering excellence and leadership among students in Egypt.
